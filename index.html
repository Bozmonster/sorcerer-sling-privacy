Sorcerer Sling – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 October 2026

This policy explains what information is handled when you play Sorcerer Sling (Android package dev.dzoft.sorcerorsling), a game published by Laser Lance ("we", "us").

Summary

  • The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no online multiplayer. Two-player mode is played on one device.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location or any files from your device.
  • Your progress, coins, purchases and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The game shows advertisements through Google AdMob. Google collects some device and usage data to deliver and measure those ads, as described below.

Information stored on your device

Game progress, stars, coins, unlocked items and settings are saved locally on your device. We cannot see this data and it is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to show rewarded ads that you choose to watch and occasional full-screen ads. When an ad is requested or shown, Google may automatically collect and process:

DataWhy
Advertising ID and other device or app identifiersServing and personalising ads, limiting how often an ad is shown, fraud prevention, analytics
IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location (country or city)Serving relevant ads, fraud prevention, security
Device information (model, operating system, language, screen size, network type)Formatting and delivering ads, diagnostics
Ad interactions (ads shown, tapped or watched) and app interactions related to adsMeasuring ad performance, analytics
Diagnostic data (crash and performance data from the ads SDK)Keeping the ads SDK working reliably

This data is collected by Google, not stored by us, and is transferred encrypted. Google's use of it is described in Google's Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices

  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your device's settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • The game can be played offline. No ads are loaded while your device is offline.

Purchases

If the game offers in-app purchases, payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We never receive your payment card details.

Children

Sorcerer Sling is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help.

Data retention and deletion

We do not hold personal data about players on our own servers, so there is nothing for us to delete. Data saved on your device is removed when you uninstall the game. For data held by Google in connection with advertising, use the controls in your Google account and device settings, or contact us and we will point you to the right place.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
