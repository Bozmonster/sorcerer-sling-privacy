Sorcerer Sling – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 October 2026 (previous version: 13 October 2026)

This policy explains what information is handled when you play Sorcerer Sling (Android package dev.dzoft.sorcerorsling), a game published by Laser Lance, a trading name of Dzoft Limited, New Zealand ("we", "us"). It adds game-specific detail to the Laser Lance Privacy Policy; use of the game is covered by the Laser Lance Terms of Service.

Summary

  • The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no online multiplayer. Two-player mode is played on one device.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location or any files from your device.
  • The game creates a random, anonymous player ID on your device. Your progress, unlocked items and purchase history are saved on our servers under that ID so they can be restored, and the shop and level lists are downloaded from our servers.
  • We collect gameplay analytics (for example which levels are started, won or lost) to improve the game. You can switch this off in Options.
  • You can erase everything we hold about you at any time with Options → Delete My Data.
  • The game shows advertisements through Google AdMob. Google collects some device and usage data to deliver and measure those ads, as described below.

How to delete your data

This applies to Sorcerer Sling by Laser Lance on Google Play.

  1. Open Sorcerer Sling and tap Options on the main menu.
  2. Tap Delete My Data, then tap it again to confirm. You need an internet connection; the row shows "Data Deleted" when done.

Or email sorcerorsling@laserlance.games with the subject "Sorcerer Sling data deletion" and roughly when you installed the game; we will delete your records within 30 days.

What is deleted: your player record (balances, equipped items, platform and app version), purchase history, unlocked items and achievements, level progress, and all gameplay analytics linked to your player ID. Nothing is kept afterwards except routine encrypted database backups, which expire within 7 days.

What is not deleted: the save on your own device (uninstall the game or clear its storage to remove it), payment records held by Google Play, and advertising data held by Google (manage it in your Google account and device settings).

Information we collect and store

All of it is linked only to the anonymous player ID, never to your name, email or advertising ID. It is sent encrypted (HTTPS) and stored in a Microsoft Azure SQL database in Australia (Australia East region), operated for us by Microsoft as our hosting provider.

Your choices

  • Share Gameplay Data (in Options): untick it and the game stops collecting and sending analytics events, and clears any it had not sent yet.
  • Delete My Data (in Options, tap twice): immediately deletes your player record, purchase history, unlocks, progress and all analytics we hold for your player ID, then gives the game a new ID. The save on your device is kept.
  • You can also email us to delete your data or to receive a copy of it.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your device's settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads). Players in the EEA and UK can change their ad consent choice in Options → Privacy Options.
  • The game can be played offline. No ads are loaded and nothing is sent to us while your device is offline; queued data is sent when you are next online.

Purchases

In-app purchases are paid through Google Play, which handles the payment. We receive only the order number, item and price, which we keep in your purchase history. We never receive your payment card details, billing name or address.

Children

Sorcerer Sling is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Data retention and deletion

  • Gameplay analytics are deleted automatically after 13 months.
  • Your player record, progress, unlocks and purchase history are deleted automatically 24 months after you last played, or straight away when you use Delete My Data or ask us by email.
  • Data saved on your device is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its storage.
  • For data held by Google in connection with advertising, use the controls in your Google account and device settings.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020, the EU/UK GDPR or the CCPA) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your information and to object to its use. We process game records to provide the game to you and analytics on the basis of our legitimate interest in improving the game, which you can object to by switching analytics off. Contact us to make a request; you can also complain to your local privacy regulator.
